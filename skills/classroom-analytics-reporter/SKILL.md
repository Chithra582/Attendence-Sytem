# Classroom Analytics Reporter Skill

## Overview
Synthesizes weekly and monthly attendance data into structured reports for academic compliance and parent-teacher conferences.

## Operations
1. Aggregates multi-week attendance ledgers.
2. Identifies day-of-week attendance variances (e.g., Friday absence spikes).
3. Produces export-ready markdown tables and compliance summaries.
