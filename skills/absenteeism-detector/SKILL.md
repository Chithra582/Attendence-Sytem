# Absenteeism Detector Skill

## Overview
Analyzes multi-session attendance logs to detect students falling below the 80% attendance threshold or accumulating consecutive unexcused absences.

## Operations
1. Evaluates student attendance history across available sessions.
2. Identifies students with 3 or more consecutive unexcused absences.
3. Flags at-risk learners and recommends timely educational intervention.
